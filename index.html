            Welcome to Folio

            Open a document or a folder and read it like a book. Your files stay on your computer.

            This browser prototype reads files you choose inside this tab only. Nothing is uploaded. A browser can't reopen your files automatically next time, and it can only open folders you pick yourself. The desktop app is planned to remember them.

            Privacy, security and help

            How Folio is designed to protect your documents, and what this prototype does and doesn't do yet.

            Read-only

            Folio opens documents for reading only. It never changes, moves or deletes your files.

            Prototype: there is no way to edit files here at all.

            Nothing is uploaded

            Your documents are read on your computer and are never sent anywhere.

            Prototype: files you open are read inside this browser tab and are not sent anywhere.

            No usage tracking

            Folio doesn't collect statistics about what you read or how you use it.

            Prototype: no tracking code. It does download fonts and display libraries from public servers when the page first opens. The desktop app would include these.

            Works offline

            Reading, searching and navigating don't need an internet connection.

            Prototype: once loaded, everything except diagrams and fonts works without a connection.

            Links to websites

            Links to other websites open in your usual web browser, never inside Folio. That keeps websites separate from your documents.

            Pictures from the internet

            Some documents include pictures stored on other websites. Loading one tells that website that you're reading, and when. Folio hides these pictures until you say otherwise.

            History and reset

            Folio remembers recent documents, searches, reading progress and your open tabs on this device only.

            Common questions

            What kind of documents can Folio open?

            Plain-text documents written in Markdown, a simple way of adding headings, lists and links to text. Their names usually end in .md. You don't need to know Markdown to read them; Folio shows them formatted.

            Why is a link shown in red with a dotted underline?

            It points to a page that isn't in this collection. The page may have been renamed, moved or not written yet. Selecting it explains what's missing.

            Why does Folio ask before showing some pictures?

            Those pictures are stored on other websites. Showing them would tell those websites that you're reading the document. You decide whether that's acceptable.

            Can I edit my documents in Folio?

            No. Folio is a reader. Keeping it read-only means it can never damage your files.

            Where are my favorites and bookmarks stored?

            On this device only, in this browser. Clearing your browser data removes them. Use "Clear history" or "Reset workspace" above to remove them yourself.
